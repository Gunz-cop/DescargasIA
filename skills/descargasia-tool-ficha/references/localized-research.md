# Localized Research

## Evidence Labels

Use one or more precise labels per query. Never inflate a label into a numeric or causal claim.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed volume | A market-specific volume value from an available keyword tool, with tool and date recorded |
| Trend | A time-series or rising/falling pattern, with source, market, and period |
| Search suggestion | Autocomplete, related-search, or marketplace suggestion; useful for wording, not demand proof |
| Qualitative evidence | SERP observations, forum wording, support questions, or repeated confusion; useful context, not volume |

## Query Clusters

Generate native wording for download/access, installation/platform, use, alternatives, privacy/account/data, offline/local, and route type. Explore the terms people actually use in that market before expanding variants.

Examples to investigate, not pre-approved candidates or demand claims:

| Market | Download/access | Route/constraint | Intent-led guide seed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spanish (Spain) | `descargar [herramienta]`, `[herramienta] para Windows` | `app oficial`, `sin instalar`, `funciona sin conexión` | `cómo usar [tipo de herramienta] sin instalar programas` |
| Italian (Italy) | `scaricare [strumento]`, `[strumento] per Windows` | `app ufficiale`, `senza installare`, `offline` | `come usare [tipo di strumento] senza installare programmi` |
| Swedish (Sweden) | `ladda ner [verktyg]`, `[verktyg] för Windows` | `officiell app`, `utan installation`, `offline` | `hur man använder [verktygstyp] utan att installera program` |

Verify which terms are natural and supported by evidence. Do not carry a tool or keyword from one row into another market automatically.

## SERP Audit Record

For each candidate query, note the search engine/tool, market settings, date, and first-result observations:

- dominant intent;
- official source presence and rank/visibility;
- language and country fit;
- stale or incomplete information;
- confusion among app, web, extension, store, GitHub, installer, or package manager;
- clone, APK, mirror, or false-download risk;
- observable editorial competition and differentiated value possible.

## Required Opportunity Matrix

| Primary query | Native variants | Language/country | Volume or evidence | Trend | Observable difficulty/competition | Result weakness | Intent | Recommended page | Confidence | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| … | … | … | Confirmed volume / Trend / Suggestion / Qualitative | … | Low/medium/high with observation | … | … | Tool ficha / Guide / Reject | Low/medium/high | URLs + date |

Use `Reject` for non-selected candidates and add a specific reason. A candidate with low competition but no demand is not an opportunity; a tool popular in Spain is not evidence for Italy or Sweden.

## Selection Test

Select only if all are true:

1. The target market has credible demand evidence.
2. The results leave a meaningful verified editorial gap.
3. The page type matches the intent.
4. Official facts and destinations can be verified.
5. Native writing can add value without translating an existing locale.

Lower confidence, broaden research, or reject when any condition fails.
