# Editorial Writing

## Write for the Selected Market

Write directly in the language, country variant, and register defined in the market brief. Do not translate an existing ficha, title, FAQ, warning, or keyword list. Preserve facts, not phrasing.

Adapt the language to locally common product terms and actual routes: web service, app, desktop app, extension, store, GitHub, documentation, or package manager. Never imply that a web service or extension is a downloadable desktop app.

## Every Ficha Must Answer

1. What is the tool and who is it for?
2. Is it a download, web service, extension, store app, repository, or documentation-led installation?
3. Which official route exists for the user’s platform and market?
4. Which fake download, clone, or APK risk is relevant?
5. When should the reader compare alternatives?

Use the market's query evidence naturally; do not keyword-stuff or turn suggestions into claims of search volume.

## Native Examples

| Market | Native wording example | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spanish (Spain) | `descargar ChatGPT para Windows`, `app oficial`, `usar sin instalar` | Literal carry-over such as “download ChatGPT” or Latin-American wording when Spain is the target |
| Italian (Italy) | `scaricare ChatGPT per Windows`, `app ufficiale`, `usare dal browser` | Translating Spanish headings word-for-word or calling a web app an installer |
| Swedish (Sweden) | `ladda ner ChatGPT för Windows`, `officiell app`, `använda i webbläsaren` | Reusing English/Spanish search phrasing as if it were Swedish demand |

These are query-writing examples, not volume claims. Verify the actual wording and demand in the target market.

## Sections and FAQ

Write 3–5 useful sections with concrete decisions or warnings. Build FAQs from the localized research and actual product facts. Include an explicit no-installer clarification in the summary, limitations, a section, FAQ, and safety notes whenever applicable.

Do not claim privacy, legal compliance, regional availability, or pricing without current primary evidence. State uncertainty plainly.
